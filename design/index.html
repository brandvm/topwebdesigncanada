08

Keep the design useful

These rules apply through implementation and future repository edits.

Reading and interaction

  • One clear H1; logical H2/H3 structure and a keyboard skip link.
  • Visible focus; 44px design target for primary controls.
  • Stack grids and move the contents rail above the article on mobile.
  • Table overflow stays inside its labelled, keyboard-focusable region.
  • Support reduced motion and 200% zoom; preserve a readable comment panel.

Content and performance

  • 2 supplied articles; no filler content, invented authors or research dates.
  • Keep all ten agency profiles and ten FAQs per article.
  • Self-host fonts; use SVG icons and dimensioned responsive images.
  • Production budgets: 30KB compressed JS, 40KB compressed CSS, 150KB fonts per page.
  • Target Lighthouse 100; require performance median ≥95 and the other automated categories 100. Scores and field Core Web Vitals need separate measurement.
